This summary documents a review of existing data and a dynamic, natural-environment trial conducted by the speech-language pathologist to determine appropriate instructional strategies and supports for expressive communication access. It is provided to the IEP team to inform consideration of assistive technology needs under IDEA special factors. It does not replace a formal assessment where access, motor, sensory, or multidisciplinary needs are identified.
